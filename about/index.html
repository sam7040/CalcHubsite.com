About CalcHub

The CalcHub Team

CalcHub is a global website providing simple, fast and privacy-friendly online calculators for everyday calculations and planning.

Our mission

Simple, fast and privacy-friendly online calculators for everyone.

What CalcHub provides

CalcHub provides browser-based calculators covering age, calories, countdowns, percentages, dates, BMI, loan / EMI and time calculations. The calculators are designed to provide clear results without requiring an account for basic use.

How the calculators work

Calculations are designed to run in your browser. Some preferences may be stored locally in your browser. Inputs are not intentionally sent to CalcHub servers merely to perform a calculation.

Accuracy and responsible use

Calculator results are intended for general informational purposes. Results can depend on the information entered and the calculation method. For medical, financial, legal or other official decisions, use the requirements and guidance of the appropriate qualified professional or authority.

Advertising

CalcHub may use advertising services in the future to support the free website. If advertising is enabled, the Privacy Policy will describe the relevant cookies, local storage, data use and third-party advertising services.
