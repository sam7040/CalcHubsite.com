Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

CalcHub ("CalcHub", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use CalcHub and how that information is handled.

1. Information used by the calculators

CalcHub's calculators are designed to perform calculations in your browser. Information you enter into a calculator, such as a date of birth, weight, height, loan amount or other calculation values, is used to produce the requested result in your browser. We do not intentionally send these calculator inputs to a CalcHub server simply to perform the calculation.

2. Local browser storage

CalcHub may use browser local storage or similar browser functionality for preferences or basic site functionality. These technologies can remain on your device until you clear them or they expire according to your browser's behavior.

3. Contact information

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your request. We use that information to respond to you and handle the request.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

CalcHub may display advertising in the future, including advertising provided by Google AdSense. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize advertising as permitted by applicable policies and law.

Google's advertising technology can use information associated with a browser or device, including interactions with advertisements, to provide and measure advertising. Google may use cookies when its ad tags are present, including on pages where advertisements are displayed.

For information about how Google uses data when you use sites or apps that use its services, see Google's Business Data Responsibility information and Privacy Policy.

5. Consent and regional privacy requirements

Where required, CalcHub will provide an appropriate consent mechanism for advertising-related cookies, local storage and personal-data processing. Google requires publishers serving personalized ads to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland to use a Google-certified consent management platform that integrates with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

When advertising is enabled, the applicable consent choices and privacy information will be provided through the site's consent and privacy mechanisms.

6. Third-party services

CalcHub does not intentionally use third-party advertising or analytics services merely because you visit the site. If third-party services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe their relevant processing and purpose.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the website and its systems appropriately protected. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

CalcHub is a general-audience website. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through calculator inputs. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, advertising arrangements or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
